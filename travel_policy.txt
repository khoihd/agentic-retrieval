# Business Travel Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains reimbursement rules for employees traveling for
approved company business. Employees should use reasonable judgment when
making travel arrangements and should follow applicable approval and
documentation requirements.

## Flights

Employees should book economy-class airfare for domestic and
international business travel. Premium economy or business-class airfare
requires manager approval before booking unless an approved exception
applies. Employees should use preferred airlines and booking channels
when reasonably available.

## Hotels

Employees traveling to Tokyo, Japan may claim lodging expenses up to
$350 per night. Employees traveling to London, United Kingdom may claim
up to $375 per night. Employees traveling within the United States may
claim up to $300 per night unless a location-specific limit applies.
Lodging expenses above the applicable nightly limit require manager
approval. Employees should use preferred hotel vendors when reasonably
available.

## Meals

Employees may claim reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved
business travel. The standard meal reimbursement limit is $80 per day
for international travel and $65 per day for domestic travel. Alcoholic
beverages are not reimbursable. Itemized receipts are required for
individual meal expenses above $25.

## Ground Transportation

Reasonable expenses for taxis, rideshare services, public
transportation, and rental cars may be reimbursed when needed for
business travel. Employees should select economical transportation
options when practical. Luxury vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable
unless specifically approved for a documented business need.

## Receipts and Documentation

Receipts are required for lodging, airfare, rental cars, and individual
expenses above $25. Expense reports should identify the business
purpose, travel dates, and destination. Employees should submit
reimbursement requests within 30 days after completing the trip.

## Approval and Exceptions

Travel should be approved by the employee’s manager before booking when
required by the employee’s department. Expenses exceeding standard
policy limits require manager approval. Exceptions should include a
documented business justification.

## Reimbursement

Approved expenses that comply with this policy are reimbursed through
the company’s expense process. Employees are responsible for providing
accurate expense information and required supporting documentation.
